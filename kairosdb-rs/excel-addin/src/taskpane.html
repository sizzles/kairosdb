KairosDB-rs

Server-side aggregated time-series queries as Excel functions.

  • =KAIROS.QUERY(metric, start, end, [agg], [bucket], [tags], [url])
  • =KAIROS.INFO(metric, start, end, [agg], [bucket], [tags], [url])
  • =KAIROS.METRICS([prefix], [url])

Example:
=KAIROS.QUERY("sensor.temp", DATE(2020,1,1), TODAY(), "avg", "1d")

Point it at a running kairosd (default http://localhost:8080).
